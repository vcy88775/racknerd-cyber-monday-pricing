## Which tier to buy

RackNerd's own documentation makes part of this decision for you, and it's specific enough to be useful. OpenClaw wants at least 8 GB of RAM. Hermes Agent, AnythingLLM, Ollama, and LibreChat want at least 4 GB. n8n and Coolify are comfortable from 2 GB. Those numbers come from the one-click app catalogue, so they're about real workloads rather than marketing.

Away from AI stacks, the rules are simpler.

**One small site, a blog, a personal project, or a proxy box** → 1 GB. $10 to $11 a year is roughly the price of a sandwich, and 25 GB of disk holds more than most people expect.

**Two or three small sites with a database, or n8n, or a couple of Docker containers** → 2.5 GB. The 2025 price was $18.66. The extra $8 buys double the RAM, which is the resource that actually runs out first.

You're paying $17.51 more than the 6 GB tier for 2 GB of RAM and 50 GB of disk. Unless you're running OpenClaw or a similarly RAM-hungry model, the value drops off here.

## Why "I'll just buy on Cyber Monday" usually fails

Three things tend to break that plan.

**Stock doesn't replenish.** Every Black Friday plan from 2023, 2024, and 2025 is currently marked sold out or off the store. There's no mechanism visible in the tracker for a Cyber Monday restock. If this year follows the same shape, Nov 30 is the tail end of the sale rather than a second opening. That's a judgment from the pattern, not an announced policy, but the direction is consistent across three years.

**Location choice shrinks with demand.** The 2023 batch offered 9 datacenter locations. 2024 offered 5. 2025 offered 1. Whether that's deliberate or simply what remained in stock, the effect is the same: the longer you wait, the less likely you are to get Los Angeles, New York, or Seattle specifically. If a particular region matters to you, the plan page has to show it at checkout, and you should read that line before paying.

**The good ones move in minutes.** Flash sales sell out inside an hour, and the strongest plans within minutes of appearing. A five-day window sounds generous right up until the 1 GB tier disappears in the first ten minutes.

This is also why the tracker pages are worth bookmarking rather than the store page. They show real stock status and log each plan the moment it appears, which is faster than refreshing a category listing.

## FAQ

**Are the Cyber Monday deals out yet?**
No. Nothing for this year has been announced, and no plans, prices, or location counts exist publicly. Anyone quoting a specific 2026 price for a specific plan is guessing.

**Is Cyber Monday cheaper than Black Friday here?**
There's no evidence of two separate price lists. The calendar treats Cyber Monday as the tail of the Black Friday event, and no year on record shows a second, deeper cut. Buying early in the window has historically been the safer bet.

**Do I need a promo code?**
No. RackNerd's promotions take the form of a directly listed price, like $10.60/year, not a coupon applied at checkout. I found no verifiable official code for this sale, and the coupon-aggregator sites circulating 50%, 58%, and 70% claims contradict each other within the same page. Ignore them.

**Will the price stay the same when I renew?**
Community users in hosting forums report that the big Black Friday deals are usually recurring, so you keep the low rate on renewal. That's user chatter, not a published term. Check the renewal amount shown in your cart before you pay, because a $10.60 first year followed by a $21.99 renewal is a different purchase than it looks.

**How many locations will there be this year?**
Unknown. The trend went 9, then 5, then 1. Plan for the possibility that the cheapest plan is single-location.
